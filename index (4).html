Revenue and Disaster Management Department

Ensuring equitable land administration and swift disaster response across Tamil Nadu.

Government of Tamil Nadu  Ramanathapuram

📖 Overview

The Revenue and Disaster Management Department is one of the most important departments of the Government of Tamil Nadu. It oversees land administration, revenue collection, and disaster management operations across the state.

🎯 Mission

To provide efficient, transparent, and citizen-centric revenue administration services while ensuring timely disaster preparedness and relief operations for the people of Tamil Nadu.

📌 Key Responsibilities

  • Administration and maintenance of land records (Patta, Chitta, A-Register)
  • Collection of land revenue and other government dues
  • Disaster relief coordination and fund distribution
  • Issuance of community, income, and residence certificates
  • Regulation of encroachment on government lands

✅ Online Patta Transfer ✅ Community Certificate ✅ Income Certificate ✅ Nativity Certificate ✅ Disaster Relief Assistance ✅ Land Survey Records ✅ FMB Sketch Online

About Us

Department of Artificial Intelligence

🤖 Our Mission

The Department of Artificial Intelligence, Ramanathapuram District, is a pioneering initiative of the Government of Tamil Nadu to integrate AI into public administration. Our mission is to leverage artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data science to improve governance, streamline citizen services, and promote transparent, accountable public administration.

🌐 Digital Tamil Nadu Vision

Aligned with the Tamil Nadu Digital Transformation Policy 2025, we are committed to creating a digitally empowered citizenry. From AI-powered chatbots for grievance redressal to predictive analytics for disaster management, our department leads the charge in public sector innovation.

📍 About Ramanathapuram

Ramanathapuram, popularly known as Ramnad, is a historic coastal district in Tamil Nadu. It is known for the Ramanathaswamy Temple at Rameswaram — one of the 12 Jyotirlinga temples — and is an important cultural and economic centre of southeastern Tamil Nadu. The district is home to approximately 13.5 lakh people spread across 8 taluks.
